About Me

Hi, I’m Rebecca Harvey. I am an industrial design student focused on creating thoughtful, functional, and visually engaging experiences that center around human needs. My work explores the relationship between form, material, and human interaction.

I am especially interested in design research, where I can combine storytelling with usability. My favorite part of design is understanding people and the way a product can bring them solutions and delight.
