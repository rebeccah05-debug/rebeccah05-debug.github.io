About Me

Hi, I’m Rebecca Harvey. I am a designer focused on creating thoughtful, functional, and visually engaging experiences. My work explores the relationship between form, material, and human interaction.

I am especially interested in product and spatial design, where I can combine storytelling with usability. My projects often begin with sketching and prototyping, then evolve through digital modeling and iterative testing.

This portfolio showcases my process and finished work, including concepts like my campfire conversation chair, which is designed to encourage connection and shared experiences.
